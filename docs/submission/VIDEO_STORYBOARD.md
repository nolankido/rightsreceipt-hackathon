# RightsReceipt Final Demo Storyboard

**Target duration:** 2:35 to 2:50  
**Hard limit:** under 3:00  
**Record from:** the frozen release candidate only

**Claim-to-screen guide:** [Judge evidence matrix and filming handoff](JUDGING_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md) maps the official judging criteria to real product surfaces and provides separate narration rules for genuine PayPal Sandbox proof and locally simulated payment.

After exporting the final MP4, run the [read-only video release QA](VIDEO_RELEASE_QA.md) against its exact path. This checks the strict three-minute boundary and presence of audio/video tracks before the manual watch-through and signed-out YouTube playback test.
If local FFmpeg is already installed, its additional `--decode-check` can independently test codec-level playback without uploading or writing another video. It does not replace listening to the narration or watching every scene.

The video should demonstrate the product, not narrate the engineering backlog. Keep the viewer oriented around one licensing request and the authority boundaries.

**Critical two-system capture rule:** The ordinary localhost judge console executes application handlers with scripted participants and injected PayPal-like responses, but does NOT perform live learned-model inference or a genuine Sandbox payment. Do not record only that console and describe it as a real AI-plus-PayPal run. For the AI segment, separately capture actual classifier diagnostics from the running creator extraction view or a verified portable-model evaluation. For the financial segment, use genuine Sandbox footage only after it exists; otherwise label all payment footage **LOCAL SIMULATION, NO REAL PAYPAL PAYMENT**. A simulated clip cannot close the genuine Sandbox acceptance gate.
